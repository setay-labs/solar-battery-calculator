📐 Sizing Formulas & Engineering References

This calculator employs industry-standard off-grid sizing methodology recommended by NABCEP and ABYC standards:

  • Adjusted Daily Load: Daily Wh ÷ Inverter Efficiency (%)
  • Total Usable Battery Wh: Adjusted Daily Wh × Days of Autonomy
  • Total Nominal Battery Wh: Usable Battery Wh ÷ Usable Depth of Discharge (DoD)
  • Battery Bank Capacity (Ah): Nominal Battery Wh ÷ System Nominal Voltage
  • Minimum Solar Panel Watts: Adjusted Daily Wh ÷ (Peak Sun Hours × Solar Derate Factor)
  • MPPT Controller Rating: Minimum Solar Watts ÷ Nominal Battery Voltage × 1.25 Safety Margin

Note: For sub-zero winter camping with LiFePO4 batteries, ensure your cells have built-in low-temperature charging protection or heating pads.
